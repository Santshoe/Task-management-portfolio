## 🔄 Workflow

My task-management approach follows a simple process:

**Capture → Prioritize → Schedule → Execute → Track → Follow Up → Complete**

### 1. Capture

I record new tasks as soon as they are identified to reduce the risk of missed responsibilities.

### 2. Prioritize

Tasks are categorized according to urgency, importance, deadlines, and business impact.

### 3. Schedule

Due dates and, where necessary, recurring schedules are assigned to ensure work is completed on time.

### 4. Execute

Tasks are worked through according to priority while maintaining attention to detail.

### 5. Track

I monitor task status to identify completed, ongoing, and outstanding work.

### 6. Follow Up

Outstanding tasks are reviewed and followed up before they become overdue.

### 7. Complete

Completed tasks are marked accordingly, creating a clear record of progress and accountability.
